Used or remanufactured?

They suit different situations, and the cheaper one is not always the right call. Here is the short version.

Used

A tested unit pulled from a donor vehicle

  • Lower cost, usually by a wide margin
  • Original factory assembly, with its original wear
  • Mileage varies, and we tell you what it is
  • Best when the rest of the vehicle has limited life left
Remanufactured

Stripped, machined and rebuilt to specification

  • Wear parts replaced, not just inspected
  • Built back to a published tolerance
  • Longer coverage than the equivalent used unit
  • Best when you intend to keep the vehicle
