About us

One company, and we will tell you which one

LifeLong Auto Parts sells replacement engines, transmissions, transfer cases, differentials and air conditioning systems to drivers and independent repair shops across the Western United States.

How we relate to Parts And Pros

We are the Western U.S. division of Parts And Pros, an established business in Washington, DC. They handle sourcing, sales fulfilment and shipping. We handle the Western market.

We say that on every page of this site, in the footer, because in this industry the opposite is common. When we researched the sellers you are likely comparing us against, we found operations trading under more than a dozen different names from a single address — which makes it very hard to work out who you are actually dealing with, or to find out what happened to the last customer who had a problem. We would rather be one name you can look up.

We do not have a West Coast warehouse

We are a Western U.S. sales division. Parts ship from the Parts And Pros facility in Washington, DC - we do not operate a West Coast warehouse. We mention it because some sellers imply a local depot they do not have, and you deserve to know where your part is coming from before you order it, not after.

What we are trying to do differently

Every seller in this category advertises a warranty. Almost none of them will tell you what it pays. We looked at the complaints against the sellers with the worst ratings and they all said the same thing: the labour claim did not get paid, or it got paid months late.

Length of warranty turns out to be a poor guide. The seller advertising seven years scores 2.0 with independent reviewers. The seller advertising the shortest warranty in the segment scores 4.5 across thousands of reviews. What separates them is not the number of years — it is whether the claim gets paid.

So we publish the parts of a warranty that actually decide that: the labour reimbursement rate, the total cap, the claim turnaround, and the exclusions. On the page, before you buy. You can read them on our warranty page.
