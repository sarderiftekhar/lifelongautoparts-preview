Used and remanufactured differentials

A differential that howls, whines under load, or has started dropping metal into its oil is on borrowed time. We supply used and rebuilt front and rear differentials, matched on ratio and spline count so the unit works with the rest of your drivetrain.

What we supply

Used differentials are supplied as complete assemblies or as centre sections, inspected for gear wear and backlash.

Rebuilt units get new bearings, seals and, where required, a new crown wheel and pinion set, then are set up to the correct backlash and pattern. A differential that is not set up properly will be noisy from the day it is fitted, so this step matters more than it sounds.

Limited-slip and locking differentials are available for the applications that had them from the factory.

Getting the fitment right

Differentials must match on gear ratio and axle spline count, and on a four-wheel drive vehicle the front and rear ratios must match each other or the drivetrain will bind. The ratio is usually on a tag on the differential cover or on the vehicle certification label. If you are replacing only one end, tell us - we will check the other end matches before shipping anything.

Common questions

How do I find my gear ratio?

Check the tag on the differential cover, or the axle code on the vehicle certification label in the door jamb. If neither is legible, count driveshaft rotations against one wheel revolution, or send us the VIN and we will look it up.

Does the front ratio have to match the rear?

On a four-wheel drive vehicle, yes. Mismatched ratios cause driveline binding and will destroy the transfer case. This is one of the most common and most expensive mistakes in this category.

Can I fit just the centre section?

On many vehicles yes, and it is cheaper. On others the differential is integral to the axle housing. Tell us the vehicle and we will say which yours is.
