Warranty

If something fails, here is what happens

Nobody reads a claims procedure before they need one. So this is written for someone reading it today, with a vehicle that has stopped, who wants to know what to do in the next hour.

Call us before anything is removed from the vehicle

Not after your shop has stripped it. Not after the diagnosis. Before. A unit that has already been dismantled is much harder to assess, and across this industry it is the single most common reason a claim gets refused. One phone call avoids it entirely.

Have this ready when you call

None of it is hard to find, and having it to hand turns a twenty-minute call into a five-minute one.

  • Your original invoice or order number from us
  • The installation invoice from the shop that fitted it, with the date and the mileage
  • The current mileage on the vehicle
  • The name and phone number of the repair facility holding the vehicle
  • A plain description of what it is doing, and when it started

Missing the installation invoice? Call anyway. Your shop will have a copy, and we would rather help you find it than turn you away over paperwork.

The process, in order

Five steps. Each one says what we do, and why it matters to you rather than to us.

  1. 1

    Call us before anything is removed

    This is the step that decides how the rest goes. Ring us while the unit is still in the vehicle, and before your shop starts dismantling anything. We give you a claim number on that call.

    A unit that has already been stripped is far harder to assess, and across this industry it is the most common reason a claim gets refused. It costs you one phone call to avoid.

  2. 2

    We authorise diagnosis

    We ask your repair facility to establish what has failed and report back. You choose the shop - it has to be licensed, but it does not have to be one of ours.

    Diagnostic time is generally outside the labour allowance, so agree with your shop up front who is paying for that hour if the claim is not upheld.

  3. 3

    The shop reports and we approve

    If the unit failed within the terms, we authorise a replacement and tell you when it ships. If it did not, we tell you why, in writing, and what your options are.

    You get a decision with a reason attached. A refusal you cannot interrogate is not a decision, it is a brush-off.

  4. 4

    The replacement is fitted

    Same process as the original installation. Keep the new installation invoice - it carries the remaining cover forward.

    The clock does not restart. Cover continues from your original activation date.

  5. 5

    Labour is reimbursed

    Paid to you or direct to the shop, whichever you prefer. Tell us which when you open the claim rather than after the work is done.

    Paying the shop direct means you are never out of pocket waiting for us, which is the complaint people actually have about this industry.

What a claim does not cover

These are the costs that surprise people mid-claim, which is the worst possible moment to learn about them. Better here.

Freight, both directions

Returning the failed unit and shipping the replacement. Ask us for the figure when you open the claim so it is not a surprise.

Towing

Getting the vehicle to the shop. If your insurance or breakdown cover includes recovery, use that.

Rental or courtesy car

We do not reimburse hire while the work is done.

Diagnostic time

The hours spent establishing what failed, as distinct from replacing it.

Consequential damage

Damage to other components caused by the failure, or by continuing to drive after it. This is why step one matters - stop driving it.

Installation by anyone unqualified

Fitting must be by a licensed repair facility and you must keep the invoice. A self-installed unit is not covered.

If we turn a claim down

It happens, and when it does you should not have to guess why or wonder what to do next. This is the escalation path, published for the same reason as everything else on this site.

  1. 1 Ask for the reason in writing, with the clause it relies on. You are entitled to both.
  2. 2 Read that clause against the written warranty you were given before purchase. If you do not have a copy, ask us and we will send one free.
  3. 3 If you think we have read it wrongly, say so and ask for it to be reviewed by someone else here. Tell us what you think we have got wrong.
  4. 4 If you are still not satisfied, you retain every right you had before you contacted us - including your rights under California law, which we cannot sign away and have not tried to.

Your statutory rights are unaffected

Nothing in our warranty removes a right you have under California or federal law. A written warranty sits on top of those rights; it does not replace them.
