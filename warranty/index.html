What we don't cover - and why we're telling you now

These are the exclusions that generate most of the complaints against this industry. You should know about them before you buy, not when you claim.

Freight on a warranty claim

Shipping the failed unit back and the replacement out. Ask us who pays this before you buy - it is the single most common surprise in this industry.

Towing

Getting the vehicle to the shop is not covered. If your policy or breakdown cover includes it, use that.

Rental or loan car

We do not reimburse a hire car while the work is done.

Diagnostic time

The hours spent establishing that the unit failed, as opposed to replacing it, are usually outside the labour allowance.

Consequential damage

Damage to other components caused by the failure, or by continuing to drive after it. This is why the activation and inspection steps matter.

Fitting by anyone unqualified

Installation has to be by a licensed repair facility, with the invoice retained. A self-installed unit will not be covered.

Your transmission fails at month 14

Here is exactly what happens, day by day, and what we pay. Same process for every part.

  1. Day 0

    You call us, before anything is removed

    This matters. A unit stripped before we authorise the claim is much harder to assess, and it is the most common reason a claim in this industry gets disputed.

  2. Day 0-1

    We authorise diagnosis at your repair facility

    You use a licensed shop of your choosing. We give you a claim number before any work starts.

  3. Day 1-3

    The shop reports, we approve the replacement

    If the unit failed within terms, we ship a replacement. You keep the original installation invoice - we will ask for it.

About lifetime cover

Our name is LifeLong, so it is fair to ask. Lifetime cover on any part requires an annual inspection at an authorised centre, and we are not publishing a lifetime claim for the Western U.S. until we can confirm those centres exist in your state. We would rather say that plainly than advertise cover you could not actually use.
